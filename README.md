<a name="about"></a>

<div align="center">

📌 ABOUT CYBERMAZE

Discover the world behind the maze.

</div>

<br/>

CyberMaze is an immersive and challenging browser-based maze game where your ultimate objective is to outsmart the labyrinth, overcome cybersecurity obstacles, and defeat the final threat before time runs out.

Navigate through complex digital corridors, solve security-themed puzzles, and conquer the maze to achieve victory!

<br/><br/>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<div align="center">

❗ THE PROBLEM

</div>

Traditional mazes can feel repetitive and lack a compelling objective. Players need more engaging experiences that combine entertainment with logical thinking.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<div align="center">

💡 OUR SOLUTION

</div>

CyberMaze transforms the classic maze into a dynamic, tech-themed adventure with strategic obstacles, security challenges, and an exciting final objective.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
